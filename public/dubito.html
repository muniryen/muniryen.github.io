ABOUT THE PROJECT

Find the best book prices worldwide, track your reading with a smart library app.

Looking for the best deals on books? Dubito – Book Prices & Library helps you compare prices across online bookstores worldwide and manage your personal reading collection in one place.

Dubito instantly compares book prices—including shipping costs—so you always find the cheapest option. Add your favorite books, and we’ll show you where to buy them at the lowest total price.

Keep track of every book you own or want to read:

📚 Library Manager – Create shelves for Read, Currently Reading, and Want to Read.

❤️ Favorites List – Save books you love and get updated price comparisons.

🔍 Smart Book Search – Quickly find titles across multiple stores worldwide.

Why Choose Dubito?

🌍 Compare book prices globally.

💰 Find cheap books & exclusive deals.

✨ Build your personal reading tracker & library.

⏱ Save time switching between bookstores.

Dubito is perfect for book lovers who want to spend less time shopping and more time reading. Start building your dream library and never overpay for books again!
