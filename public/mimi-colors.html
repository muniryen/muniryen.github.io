ABOUT THE GAME

More colors.
A bigger challenge.

Find the repeated color in a randomly colored grid as quickly as possible. Selecting one of the matching cells is enough. Six levels, each with 10 questions, take you from a 2×2 grid to a 7×7 challenge.
