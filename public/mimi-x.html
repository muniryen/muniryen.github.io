MiMi X Space Defence

Aim, launch and match colorful shapes in a playful space defence adventure!

Google Play ↗ Privacy Policy ↗

Get ready for a colorful cosmic challenge in MiMiX - Space Defence!

Pilot your pink spaceship through a playful universe of smiling shapes, sparkling explosions and moving stars. Aim your shots, connect matching shapes and keep the rising pressure under control as new rows descend from above.

THREE SHAPES, THREE WAYS TO MATCH

Connect at least 3 triangles, 4 squares or 5 circles to make them pop! Matching shapes can connect horizontally, vertically or diagonally. When a successful match leaves other shapes disconnected from the top, those shapes burst too.

AIM, RELEASE, POP!

Drag left or right anywhere in the play area to adjust your aim, then lift your finger to fire. Take a look at the next shape and plan ahead—but keep an eye on the countdown! When time runs out, your spaceship fires automatically.

CHOOSE YOUR BOARD

Play on a compact 6×9 board, explore the 8×12 layout or take on the spacious 10×15 grid. Each board keeps its own best score and saved game, so you can return to your favorite format and continue where you left off.

BUILD YOUR SPACE TOOLKIT

Earn XP and gold as you clear shapes. Level up to unlock shop items and spend your gold on upgrades:

  • Longer aiming guides to help you plan shots and wall bounces.
  • Shape bombs that clear every triangle, square or circle of the matching type.
  • More time to aim before automatic shots.
  • Extra time between incoming rows.

CLEAR THE BOARD, GROW YOUR REWARD

Pop every shape on the board to trigger a celebration and earn bonus gold. Each full-board clear makes the next clear bonus even bigger!

KEEP THE SHAPES AT BAY

New rows arrive faster as your run progresses. A red alert warns you when shapes enter the bottom three rows. Keep matching and clearing—reaching the final row ends your run.

ONE MORE CHANCE

When a rewarded ad is available, watch it after a loss to clear the bottom half of the board and get back into the action.

MAKE IT YOURS

Enjoy colorful space visuals, rotating shapes and cheerful effects. Adjust music and sound effects independently, and choose from multiple languages.

Ready to aim for a new personal best? Launch into MiMiX - Space Defence!
