MiMi Party

Catch the right moment. Complete the letters. Start the party!

Open on Google Play ↗Privacy Policy ↗

ABOUT THE GAME

One tap.
Perfect timing.

Stop the moving red box in the right place on a 7×7 grid and build letters from A to Z. Each new level gets faster in MiMi Party, putting your hand-eye coordination and timing to the test.

How to play

  1. Press the button when the red box is inside a target marked by a thick border.
  2. Complete the targets in each row and work your way upward from the bottom.
  3. Selecting an already captured cell again undoes that selection.

Start each level with three lives. An incorrect choice costs one life and restarts the current row. When your lives run out, watch an ad to continue with three new lives or restart the level.

The moving box takes 5 seconds to complete a cycle in the first level. Each level shortens this by 120 milliseconds, reaching 2 seconds in the final level.

The inspiration behind the game

Inspired by the arcade machine in episode 25 of Var Bunlar. Adapted from the Casual Games Toolkit game_template project.

Details of the color palettes, music, sound effects, fonts and other contributions are available in the store description.
