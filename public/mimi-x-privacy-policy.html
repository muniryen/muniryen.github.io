Accounts and application data

The game does not require an account or login. Saved games, best scores for each board size, XP, gold, upgrades, and language and audio preferences are stored on your device to support progress and settings. The game does not send these saved progress records to a developer-operated server.

Advertising and third-party services

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may collect and share IP addresses, advertising/app identifiers, usage interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. The data involved depends on the SDK version and configuration. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Ads include rewarded and interstitial formats. Where required, the consent form is shown before requesting ads. Privacy choices can be revisited through the in-game privacy options when this option is required and available.

Retention and deletion

You can remove app data stored on your device by clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Backups depend on your device backup settings. This does not delete data held by advertising providers, whose retention and deletion practices are described in their policies. There is no in-app account to delete. For information you sent by email, contact the developer to request deletion.

Privacy controls

Advertising ID preferences can be managed in your Android privacy and advertising settings. See the provider documents below for more information about security, retention and privacy choices.
