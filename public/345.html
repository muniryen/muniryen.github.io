ABOUT THE PROJECT

Merge 3 triangles, 4 squares and 5 circles. Keep merging and beat your best!

Turn every swipe into a colorful puzzle with 345!

Merge triangles, squares, and circles to create new generations of shapes and build your score. Make room on the board, plan your moves, and beat your personal best with pastel colors, smooth animations, and relaxing music.

SIMPLE RULES, AN ENDLESS MERGING CYCLE • Merge 3 triangles to make a square: 3 points. • Merge 4 squares to make a circle: 12 points. • Merge 5 circles to make a triangle from the next generation: 60 points.

The cycle keeps going! New triangles become new squares, and new squares become new circles. In the next generation, these merges award 180, 720, and 3600 points respectively. Colors and marks such as I, II, and III identify each shape's generation. Match both the shape and its mark to merge tiles. Shapes from different generations can share the same board. Swipe up, down, left, or right to move the shapes. Make every empty space count as you work toward more advanced shapes! THREE BOARDS, THREE SEPARATE RECORDS • 6×6 — Hard • 7×7 — Balanced • 8×8 — Easy Every space matters on smaller boards, while larger boards give you more room to plan. Each board size has its own saved game, score, best score, and highest shape reached. Switch between boards, view your records and highest shapes on the home screen, and pick up your saved game where you left off. Your best score and highest shape record stay with that board when you start a new run. RETHINK YOUR MOVES Start every run with 3 free undos. When you run out, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for 2 extra undos. If no moves remain, a rewarded ad can clear the bottom two rows and give you a chance to continue your run. PLAY YOUR WAY • Bright pastel colors and a clean interface • Ghost trails and merge animations • Colorful points that fly to the score display • Separate saves, records, and highest shapes for each board • Relaxing music and soft sound effects • Music, sound effect, vibration, and reduced motion options • Portrait and landscape support • A short tutorial to guide your first moves Choose your board, merge your shapes, and discover how far you can take the cycle!
