Accounts and application data

The game can be played without an account or login. Apart from the third-party services described below, the developer does not directly collect, process, share or host user data.

Advertising and third-party services

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may collect and share IP addresses, advertising/app identifiers, usage interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. The data involved depends on the SDK version and configuration. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

The existing app policy also discloses Google Play Games. It collects activity, usage, performance, personal and device identifiers for functionality, account management, analytics and debugging. Play Games data is encrypted in transit.

Retention and deletion

See the provider documents below for retention and deletion practices. Clearing app storage in Android removes local app data, but does not delete provider-held data. Contact the developer with privacy enquiries.

Privacy controls

Advertising ID preferences can be managed in your Android privacy and advertising settings. See the provider documents below for more information about security, retention and privacy choices.
